# Data

Dataset provenance, the modeling population funnel, split construction,
and where each artifact lives.

## Source

`data/raw/loan_data_2007_2014.csv`: the Lending Club public loan tape,
466,285 issued loans, origination dates 2007-06 through 2014-12. Each
row is a funded loan with application-time borrower/bureau fields,
pricing fields assigned at funding, and full repayment history through
charge-off or payoff.

## Label construction

Binary target:

- GOOD (0): `Fully Paid` and related fully-paid statuses.
- BAD (1): `Charged Off`, `Default`, and late statuses (31–120 days).
- `Current` loans are not dropped outright; they are eligible only if
  they satisfy the performance-window maturity rule below.

## Performance window

M = 33 months, the 90th percentile of months-to-bad observed on the
mature 2011–2012 cohort. A loan's outcome is only trusted once it has
had M months to season:

- Bad and resolved-good loans are always eligible.
- `Current` loans must have age ≥ M at the snapshot date.
- The safe-horizon cutoff (snapshot 2016-01-01 minus M) removes young
  vintages whose outcomes are still unresolved.

Implementation: `src/performance_window.py`.

## Population funnel

| Stage | Rows |
|---|---|
| Raw tape | 466,285 |
| Eligible after dedup, date parsing, performance window | ~253,282 |
| Canonical modeling set (Scenario A feature selection) | 117,841 |

Bad rate in the modeling set is roughly 14–15%, which is why class
imbalance was studied explicitly (`experiments/reports/class_imbalance_sampling.md`).

## Splits (temporal, no random splits anywhere)

| Split | Period | Rows (approx.) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Train | 2007-08 to 2012-11 | 84,764 | fit + early stopping |
| Validation | latest ~11% of history | 10,596 | model selection, calibration, τ |
| OOT test | 2013 Q1 | 22,481 | final evaluation, touched once |

Inside train, an 85/15 split separates the fit set from the
early-stopping/calibration set (50/50 of the 15%). The OOT pickles
(`X_test_raw.pkl`, `y_test.pkl`) are never loaded by
`autoresearch/experiment.py` or by threshold tuning.

## Feature eligibility

Scenario A (`pipeline/scenario_a/features.py`): application-time fields
only. Excludes everything assigned at or after funding.

Scenario B (`pipeline/scenario_b/features.py`): adds funding and pricing
fields plus derived ratios. Still excludes repayment/collection outcomes.

Both specs hard-reject leakage columns at scoring time; see
`docs/method.md` for the full module map.

## Known data limitations

- **Funded-only population.** The tape contains issued loans; rejected
  applicants are absent. Scenario A PDs are therefore optimistic for the
  true applicant pool. `experiments/reject_inference.py` and
  `experiments/reports/reject_inference.md` bound this bias (plausible applicant-pool
  PD 1.4–1.9× the funded estimate).
- **Single OOT window.** 2013Q1 is one quarter; temporal stability is
  asserted from one snapshot, not a rolling series.
- **Lending Club platform bias.** The tape reflects one originator's
  underwriting and securitization-era mix (2007–2014); generalization to
  other lenders or vintages is untested.
